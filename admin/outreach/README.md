## 🎯 Outreach Goals

Successful outreach should:
- ✅ Reach target audience (grad students, postdocs, faculty)
- ✅ Clearly communicate workshop content and value
- ✅ Generate sufficient registrations (aim for 120-150% of capacity)
- ✅ Attract participants with appropriate background
- ✅ Create excitement and anticipation
- ✅ Build long-term community around workshop series

## 📢 Promotional Materials

### Flyers

**Files**: `Example Flyer.ai` / `Example Flyer.pdf` and `Example Flyer 2.ai` / `Example Flyer 2.pdf`

**Formats**:
- `.ai` files: Adobe Illustrator source files (fully editable)
- `.pdf` files: Print-ready and digital distribution versions

**What to include on flyers**:

**Essential Information**:
- Workshop title: "Neural Decoding Workshop" (or your variant)
- Date(s) and time
- Location (building, room number)
- Registration link or QR code
- Contact email

**Compelling Content**:
- Brief description (1-2 sentences about what participants will learn)
- Key topics covered (bullet points)
- Who should attend (grad students, postdocs, etc.)
- Prerequisites (if any)
- Cost (if free, prominently state "FREE WORKSHOP")
- Registration deadline

**Visual Elements**:
- Engaging imagery (brain illustrations, neural recordings, BCI examples)
- Institutional logos
- QR code for easy registration
- High contrast for readability
- Consistent color scheme

**Example Layout**:
```
[Eye-catching image of brain/neural activity]

NEURAL DECODING WORKSHOP
Learn to analyze neural data and build brain-computer interfaces

📅 [Dates]  |  📍 [Location]  |  💰 FREE

What you'll learn:
• Neural data analysis
• Machine learning for brain decoding  
• Hands-on with real datasets
• Build your own BCI decoder

Who: Grad students, postdocs, and faculty
Prerequisites: Basic Python programming
Registration: [QR code] or [shortened URL]

[Institutional logos]
```

### Customizing Flyers

**Using Adobe Illustrator**:
1. Open `.ai` file
2. Update text with your event details
3. Modify colors to match your institution's branding
4. Add/update logos
5. Export as PDF for printing, PNG for digital use

**Without Adobe Illustrator**:
1. Use the provided PDF as a template
2. Recreate in PowerPoint, Canva, or similar
3. Maintain clear hierarchy and readability
4. Test QR codes before finalizing

**Design Tips**:
- Use large, readable fonts (minimum 12pt for body text)
- Ensure high contrast (dark text on light background or vice versa)
- Leave adequate white space
- Test readability from 6 feet away (for physical posters)
- Make sure QR codes are large enough to scan easily

## 📅 Outreach Timeline

### 3-6 Months Before Workshop

**Goals**: Build awareness, get on people's calendars

**Actions**:
- [ ] Finalize workshop dates and location
- [ ] Create initial flyer and registration form
- [ ] Send save-the-date to department heads and PIs
- [ ] Post on institutional calendars
- [ ] Reach out to program coordinators

**Communications**:
- Brief email with dates and basic info
- Request to share with students/postdocs
- Offer to give brief presentation at group meetings

---

### 2-3 Months Before Workshop

**Goals**: Drive registrations, build excitement

**Actions**:
- [ ] Full promotional push with complete flyer
- [ ] Distribute flyers physically and digitally
- [ ] Email all target groups
- [ ] Post on social media
- [ ] Submit to event calendars and newsletters
- [ ] Reach out to student organizations

**Communications**:
- Complete workshop description
- Highlight what's unique/valuable
- Include testimonials if available (from previous years)
- Emphasize limited spots if applicable

---

### 1 Month Before Workshop

**Goals**: Final push for registrations, answer questions

**Actions**:
- [ ] Send reminder emails to previous contacts
- [ ] Second round of social media posts
- [ ] Post flyers in new locations
- [ ] Reach out to waitlisted if at capacity
- [ ] Answer registration questions promptly

**Communications**:
- "Registration closing soon" messaging
- Address FAQs
- Share participant profiles (if diverse and interesting)

---

### 1-2 Weeks Before Workshop

**Goals**: Confirm attendance, provide logistics

**Actions**:
- [ ] Send confirmation/reminder to registered participants
- [ ] Provide pre-workshop setup instructions
- [ ] Share logistics (parking, directions, schedule)
- [ ] Final social media post

**Communications**:
- Welcome registered participants
- Logistics details
- What to bring
- Pre-workshop setup (Python installation, etc.)

## 🎯 Tracking Outreach Effectiveness

### Metrics to Track

**Registration form should ask**: "How did you hear about this workshop?"
- Email from department
- Flyer on campus
- Social media
- Word of mouth
- Other

**Analyze**:
- Which channels drove most registrations
- Which departments/groups had highest interest
- Timeline of registrations (did follow-ups help?)
- No-show rate vs. source

**Use insights to**:
- Focus future efforts on most effective channels
- Improve messaging based on what resonated
- Refine target audience
- Justify outreach budget if applicable

## 💡 Outreach Tips and Best Practices

### Do's ✅

- **Start early**: 3+ months lead time for academic calendars
- **Personalize**: Customize emails to different audiences
- **Follow up**: Don't assume one email is enough
- **Make registration easy**: Simple form, clear instructions
- **Build FOMO**: "Limited spots", "Waitlist available"
- **Leverage champions**: Ask faculty to share with their groups
- **Provide value clearly**: What will participants gain?
- **Be responsive**: Answer questions quickly

### Don'ts ❌

- **Don't spam**: Respect email etiquette, 2-3 contacts max
- **Don't be vague**: Unclear expectations = fewer registrations
- **Don't hide prerequisites**: Be upfront about required skills
- **Don't over-promise**: Accurately represent workshop scope
- **Don't ignore questions**: Unresponded queries = lost participants
- **Don't forget accessibility**: Mention accommodations available
